Glacier Mass-balance Modelling

Python · ERA5 · ML
  • Perfect-prognosis based Empirical-Statistical Downscaling to predict mass-balance components using ERA5 predictors.
  • PP-ESD adopted as it is RCM/GCM agnostic — flexible for future projection workflows.
  • EOF (PCA)-based pattern mining in climate fields to derive teleconnection indices (NAO, EA, SCAN).
  • Feature engineering for ML workflows, robust cross-validation & systematic feature selection.
